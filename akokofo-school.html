ABOUT AKOKOFO SCHOOL

Education Through Healing & Care

Akokofo School is an initiative by The Akili Foundation designed to provide continuous education to children undergoing cancer treatment. Our mission is to maintain intellectual engagement and offer a vital sense of normalcy despite daunting medical challenges.

Through a blend of on-site teaching at partner hospital units, tailored home tutoring, and digital learning tools, Akokofo School ensures no child's education is paused because of an illness.

Our Mission & Reach

We work closely with healthcare professionals and dedicated volunteer educators to bring interactive, uplifting learning experiences directly to pediatric oncology wards and homes.
