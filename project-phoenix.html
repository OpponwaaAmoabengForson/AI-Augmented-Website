INTRODUCTION

Who We Are & What We Are Doing

Project Phoenix is an initiative to equip convicts with digital skills. The goal is to prepare them on their reintegration journey for the 21st Century and its increasingly digital job market.

The pilot project for four months will be a parallel cohort of intro to web development and digital marketing—engaging 10 juveniles each. Project Phoenix aims to engage and equip 250 inmates with proficiency of at least one digital skill each year.

Goals & Mission

This initiative is a brainchild of Learnira, a STEM-focused start-up that believes every African child should have access to quality education. Our previous campaign (2017–2019) impacted 7,400 students across 37 public schools in Nigeria and Ghana.

Why Is This Important?

PILOT SCOPE, PROGRESS & PLAN

The 4 Learning Tracks

We are working with the Senior Correctional Centre to integrate four foundational learning tracks into existing Reintegration Programs.

01. Digital Natives

Exposes learners to the opportunities available in the digital world, netiquette, online safety, and foundational computer operations.

02. Introduction to Coding

Equips learners with practical web development skills (HTML/CSS/JS) to build real software projects and understand basic technology architecture.

03. Access to Formal Education Online

Connects inmates to accredited open-course platforms and digital learning libraries to help them resume formal academic growth.

04. Re-integration Program

Prepares participants for market realities, freelance opportunities, digital marketing roles, and mentorship opportunities upon discharge.
