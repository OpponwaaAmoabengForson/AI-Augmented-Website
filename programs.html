Project Phoenix

Project Phoenix is an initiative to equip convicts with digital skills. The goal is to prepare them on their reintegration journey for the 21st Century and its increasingly digital job market. Project Phoenix aims to engage and equip 250 inmates with proficiency of at least one digital skills each year.

Akokofo School

Children undergoing cancer treatment in Ghana are usually pulled out of school to help them complete their treatment. There is still a need for continous education while this is ongoing, this is tha gap we are filling.
